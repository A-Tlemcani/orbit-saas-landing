FAQ

Questions, answered.

Where is my data stored?

In the EU (Frankfurt and Paris) by default. Enterprise plans can pin data to a single region. We never train models on your data.

Do I have to replace my monitoring stack?

No. Orbit sits on top of Prometheus, Datadog, Grafana or OpenTelemetry and correlates what they already send.

How is a "seat" counted?

Any engineer who can acknowledge incidents. Viewers and stakeholders are free and unlimited.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly plans cancel at the end of the period; yearly plans are refunded pro rata within 30 days.
